# Glossary: 3D NAND High-Aspect-Ratio Oxide/Nitride Stack Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**a-C (amorphous carbon) mask:** Thick PECVD carbon hard mask (reference 2.4 µm with a 50 nm SiON cap) that defines through-stack features. Denser and doped variants erode more slowly. (Ch. 4.4, Ch. 13.1, App. A.5)

**A₀ (ARDE constant):** Aspect ratio at which the etch rate falls to half its open-area value in R = R₀/(1 + A/A₀). Reference 90 for holes, 120 for slits. (Ch. 3.6)

**Acceptance angle (θ_c):** Largest ion angle from the axis that still reaches the bottom of a feature without striking the wall; θ_c ≈ 1/A. (Ch. 3.5.1)

**Ammonium fluorosilicate ((NH₄)₂SiF₆):** Solid salt formed when nitride reacts with HF. It does not sublime at cryogenic wafer temperatures and must be removed by ions. (Ch. 4.6.3, App. B.1.2)

**APC (advanced process control):** Feed-forward and feedback adjustment of recipe settings from upstream and post-etch data. (Ch. 15.5–15.6)

**Arcing:** Electrical breakdown at the wafer, ESC, bevel, or ring under multi-kV bias. (Ch. 5.7)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Decline of etch rate with aspect ratio. (Ch. 3.6)

**Arcsine IED:** Bimodal ion energy distribution produced by low-frequency sinusoidal bias when ions follow the instantaneous sheath voltage. (Ch. 6.2.1)

**Aspect ratio (A):** Depth divided by mean width w̄. Reference hole 93, slit 56. (Ch. 1.2.1)

---

## B

**Bow:** Widening of a feature below the top, usually at 1–3 µm, from mask-facet reflection and wide-angle ions. (Ch. 11.2)

**Bow class:** Category of incoming wafer bow used to select the chucking sequence and He pressure. (App. C.8)

**Bottom CD:** Width of the feature at the landing interface. Reference ≥ 70 nm for holes. (Ch. 11.4)

**Bottom-placement budget:** Sum of twist, tilt, chucking, overlay, and distortion terms that move the feature bottom. (Ch. 12.5)

---

## C

**Cap oxide:** Pure oxide layer at the top of a deck (100–300 nm). (Ch. 2.5, Ch. 7.5.1)

**CCP (capacitively coupled plasma):** Parallel-plate reactor used for HAR dielectric etch, with a VHF source and LF bias. (Ch. 5.1)

**CD-SAXS:** Transmission small-angle X-ray scattering. Measures array-averaged profile, depth, modulation (superlattice peaks), and tilt. (Ch. 10.8, Ch. 15.4)

**Charging (differential):** Positive charge build-up at the bottom and negative at the top of a feature because electrons are shaded by the aspect ratio. (Ch. 12.1)

**Clausing factor (W):** Free-molecular transmission probability of a channel. Round hole ≈ 1/(1 + 3A/4); slot ≈ (ln A + 0.1)/A. (Ch. 3.5.2, App. E.3)

**Clogging (etch stop):** Feature closes or stops etching because deposition outruns ion removal at the neck or lower wall. (Ch. 11.5)

**Cryogenic etch:** Etch with the wafer at about −20 to −60 °C, typically with HF-based chemistry, relying on adsorbed etchant. (Ch. 4.6, Ch. 8.4)

---

## D

**Deck:** One stack segment etched in a single hole etch. Reference deck: 160 pairs, 8.4 µm. (Ch. 1.3.1)

**Depth series:** Set of wafers stopped at successive recipe stages to follow profile evolution. (App. C.1)

**Design space (per-deck limit):** The deck height allowed by the time, mask, bottom-CD, and twist constraints. (Ch. 14.2)

---

## E

**Effective rate (R_eff):** Stack etch rate equal to the thickness-weighted harmonic mean of the film rates, 1/(φ_ox/R_ox + φ_N/R_N). Reference 312.5 nm/min. (Ch. 3.1)

**Edge ring (focus ring):** Ring around the wafer that sets the sheath at the edge; its wear causes edge tilt. (Ch. 9.3.2)

**ESC (electrostatic chuck):** Chuck that clamps the wafer electrostatically and cools it through backside He. (Ch. 8.1.2, Ch. 8.5)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average used in feedback control. (Ch. 15.6.1)

---

## F

**Facet (mask facet):** Sloped edge of the mask opening created by angle-dependent sputtering; reflects ions into the wall. (Ch. 11.2.1)

**FDC (fault detection and classification):** Monitoring of tool signals to detect and classify faults per wafer. (Ch. 15.7)

**Feed-forward:** Adjusting the recipe from upstream measurements, such as stack thickness. Because of ARDE, Δt = ΔH/R(A_end). (Ch. 15.5)

**φ_ox, φ_N:** Oxide and nitride thickness fractions of the pair pitch. Reference 0.44 / 0.56. (Ch. 2.3.1)

**Front span:** Number of pairs crossed by the curved etch front (sag/p). Reference ≈ 0.6. (Ch. 3.4)

---

## H

**Harmonic mean:** See *effective rate*. (Ch. 3.1.2)

**HF-based chemistry:** Etch chemistry that uses HF (fed or formed in situ) as an adsorbed etchant at low temperature. (Ch. 4.6.2)

**HV-SEM:** High-voltage scanning electron microscopy used to image feature bottoms. (Ch. 15.4)

**Hydrofluorocarbon (HFC):** Gas containing H, F, and C (CH₂F₂, CHF₃, CH₃F); raises the nitride rate. (Ch. 4.2.2)

---

## I

**IDL (inter-deck layer):** Layer between decks, usually oxide, crossed by the deck-2 etch at the joint. (Ch. 2.5, Ch. 7.5.2)

**IED (ion energy distribution):** Distribution of ion energies at the wafer, set by bias frequency and waveform. (Ch. 6.2)

**Interface notching:** Grooves at oxide/nitride interfaces from graded transition zones that etch laterally faster. (Ch. 10.3.2)

**Interface transient:** Lag of the polymer layer after the front changes film, which pulls the apparent film rates together. (Ch. 3.3)

---

## J

**Joint:** Connection between a deck-2 hole bottom and the deck-1 hole top (plug). Reference allowed offset 22.5 nm. (Ch. 7.5.2)

---

## L

**Landing layer:** Layer in which the etch stops (doped poly-Si for holes; source stack for slits; metal for TAC). (Ch. 2.5, Ch. 13.2)

**Layer counting:** Endpoint by counting pair-frequency oscillations in OES. Practical only in the first ~1 µm of a HAR etch. (Ch. 15.2)

**Layer modulation (scalloping, wall steps):** Periodic horizontal ridges or steps on the wall at each ON interface. (Ch. 10)

**LS (landing step):** Selective recipe step into the landing layer. (Ch. 7.2)

---

## M

**M (damping factor):** Fraction of the pair-frequency OES amplitude that survives front spread, exp(−2π²σ_z²/p²). (Ch. 15.2.2, App. E.8)

**ME-1, ME-2, ME-3:** Main-etch stages for the upper, middle, and lower stack. (Ch. 7.1.2)

**Mask budget:** Required mask thickness from erosion, edge excess, facet allowance, and minimum remaining mask. (Ch. 13.1)

**Memory (channel) hole:** Vertical hole through a deck in which the memory films and channel are deposited. (Ch. 1.2)

---

## N

**Neck:** Local narrowing just below the top of a feature from polymer and redeposition. (Ch. 11.3)

**Non-periodic layers:** Cap, inter-deck, buffer, and landing layers that interrupt the ON pairs. (Ch. 2.5)

---

## O

**OE (overetch):** Etch time after the first features land, covering systematic and random arrival spread. Reference 4.0 min. (Ch. 13.2)

**OES (optical emission spectroscopy):** Monitoring of plasma emission for endpoint, rate, and chamber state. (Ch. 15, App. F)

**ON stack:** Alternating SiO₂ and Si₃N₄ layers of a replacement-gate 3D NAND array. (Ch. 1.1.2)

**OPOP stack:** Alternating oxide and polysilicon layers of a gate-first design. (Ch. 4.7)

---

## P

**P/T (precision-to-tolerance):** 6σ_meas divided by the specification window. (Ch. 15.4.2, App. F.5)

**Pair pitch (p):** Thickness of one oxide + nitride pair. Reference 50 nm. (Ch. 2.3.1)

**Pass fraction (f):** Fraction of ions entering a feature that reach the bottom without hitting the wall. Reference hole at 5 keV: 0.44. (Ch. 3.5.1, Ch. 6.1)

**Per-deck limit:** Maximum deck height allowed by time, mask, bottom CD, and twist. Reference 8.0–9.2 µm. (Ch. 14.2)

**Polymer-thickness model:** R = R_bare exp(−T_p/λ_p), relating etch rate to steady-state fluorocarbon film thickness. (Ch. 3.3.1)

**Pulsed bias:** Bias switched on and off at kHz rates for charge relief, passivation, and higher peak energy at the same average power. (Ch. 6.3)

---

## R

**ρ (rate ratio):** R_N/R_ox. Reference blanket value 0.91; drifts to about 0.82 at the bottom without correction. (Ch. 3.6.3)

**Rate-matching matrix:** Linearized sensitivities of oxide, nitride, and mask rates to gas and power changes. (Ch. 4.3.2, App. B.3)

**Recess (landing):** Depth the earliest features etch into the landing layer during overetch, t_OE·R_b/S. (Ch. 13.2.3)

**Replacement gate:** Flow in which sacrificial nitride is removed through the slit and replaced by word-line metal. (Ch. 1.1.3)

---

## S

**Sag:** Depth of the bowl-shaped bottom from rim to centre. Reference ≈ 30 nm. (Ch. 3.4.1)

**Seasoning:** Building a steady-state wall condition after a clean. (Ch. 9.5, App. C.4)

**Selectivity (S):** Ratio of stack rate to mask or landing-layer rate. Mask: 6.9 at the top, 3.4 at the bottom. (Ch. 4.4, Ch. 13.2)

**Sheath:** Space-charge region above the wafer across which ions are accelerated. About 10 mm thick at 4 kV in the reference. (Ch. 5.3)

**Slit (gate-line slit):** Long trench through the stack that divides blocks and gives access for nitride replacement. (Ch. 1.2)

**Stoney's equation:** Relation between film stress and substrate curvature. (Ch. 2.4.2, App. E.5)

**Support (dummy) hole:** Hole through the staircase region that holds the structure during nitride removal. (Ch. 1.2, Ch. 13.3.2)

---

## T

**T_⊥ (transverse temperature):** Effective transverse ion energy that sets the angular spread σ_θ = √(T_⊥/E). Reference 0.5 eV. (Ch. 6.1.3)

**TAC (through-array contact):** Contact etched through both decks to the CMOS below. (Ch. 1.2)

**Tailored waveform:** Non-sinusoidal bias waveform that produces a narrow IED. (Ch. 6.2.3)

**Taper:** Narrowing of a feature with depth. Reference mean angle 1.79 mrad (0.10°). (Ch. 11.4)

**Tilt:** Systematic inclination of features, mainly at the wafer edge, from edge-sheath bending or an unseated wafer. (Ch. 12.3)

**Twist (twisting):** Random, feature-to-feature deviation of the axis caused by charging. Displacement grows as depth^(3/2). (Ch. 12.2)

---

## V

**Vertical striation:** Streaks running down the wall, transferred from mask edge roughness. (Ch. 10.1)

**Virtual metrology (VM):** Per-wafer prediction of results from tool and upstream data. (Ch. 15.7.2)

**Voltage contrast (VC):** E-beam inspection that separates landed from unlanded features electrically. (Ch. 15.4)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless autoclean):** Plasma clean between wafers that resets wall polymer. (Ch. 9.2.1)

**Wall exposure time (t_exp):** Time a wall depth remains exposed after the front passes it; sets layer modulation and bow. (Ch. 10.2)

**Word line (WL):** Horizontal gate plate at one level of the array, formed by replacing a nitride layer. (Ch. 1.1.2)
